# Carbide and Carbon Building: original authored exterior

Original Blender geometry by this project, released under CC0-1.0. No photograph
pixels, third-party mesh or texture are distributed in the asset. Editable source:
`tools/blender/authored/carbide_carbon.blend`; author script:
`tools/blender/chicago_carbide_carbon.py`.

References consulted 2026-10-03:
- Chicago Architecture Center, [building article](https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/carbide-and-carbon-building).
  Full exterior/upper crown photos inspected in browser: Eric Allix Rogers credit
  accompanies the gallery. Supports black granite, green/gold terra cotta, bronze,
  plant relief, setbacks and illuminated crown. Reference photos are not included.
- [Chicago Loop photo gallery](https://thechicagoloop.org/a.ccar.00000.html),
  published 503 ft height (153.3144 m); model rounds to 153.3 m.
- Project `trackgen/data/chicago/city.json`, OSM w148544831, mapped ~39.5 x 43.9 m
  footprint centered (-50.4,-192.2), with measured roof cells clustering near 86 m.
  The mapped height tag 158 m is not used as architectural cap height.

Model: modeled four-sided office/hotel exterior, black-granite podium, inset
window/sash/spandrel geometry, continuous glazed piers, physical stylized botanical
relief, east-end stepped tower, corner pinnacles, medallion frieze, gilded cap,
recessed cap coffers/vent grille and raised Michigan Avenue entrance lettering.
Published height and mapped footprint constrain the envelope. Exact floor heights,
upper offsets, window divisions, sculpture/relief profiles and lettering placement
are approximations from the references, not surveyed/as-built documentation.
Selective occupied windows and crown/relief respond to the game's night setting.
