- Goettsch Partners, renovation architect: https://www.gpchicago.com/architecture/londonhouse-chicago/ . Existing Alfred Alschuler 1923 building, separate 2016 infill glass tower, rooftop terrace and refurbished cupola. Architect describes glass safety rails set back from historic facade. Gallery exterior, street close-up and rooftop photos inspected in browser; proportions/detail references only, not downloaded or redistributed.
- Chicago Architecture Center: https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/london-guarantee-building . Beaux-Arts Corinthian columns, carved Roman figures, ringed colonnade and domed cupola; irregular site and five-story section; Neptune relief over original entrance.
- City landmark designation: https://webapps1.chicago.gov/landmarksweb/web/landmarkdetails.htm?lanId=1357 . 360 North Michigan, Alfred S. Alschuler, 1922–23, irregular former Fort Dearborn site.
- CVU registry: https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/chicago/london-guarantee-building/10469/ . Architectural/tip 102.9 m, occupied 78.8 m, 22 floors. Architect describes existing 21-story building; floor-count difference retained rather than silently reconciled.
- Mapped footprint: OSM w147399567 in current city.json, local origin (-57.85, street y8, -351.3), no added yaw. Overall mapped 49.5 x 46.2 m. The full mapped historic footprint is replaced once; neighboring glass infill remains separately mapped.

Authored detail: a concave corner facade with individual recessed panes, sash,
limestone jambs, spandrels, rustication, cornice/dentil bands and upper giant
pilasters; arched historic entry, raised lettering and striped cafe awnings.
Cupola columns receive a restrained warm night material, separate from occupied
hotel panes. Rustication remains on stone piers rather than crossing windows.
Rooftop cupola contains stepped base, eight separate columns, capitals, open
colonnade with clear enclosure, ringed entablature, drum, physical dome and finial.
Setback transparent terrace screens, handrails, a few planters and seats modeled.

Photo-derived approximation: corner sag 3.5 m along mapped diagonal chord, main
roof 83.2 m, cupola profile/radius/placement, 22 displayed facade rows and ornamental
spacing. Only overall 102.9 m height and mapped envelope are source dimensions;
these intermediate proportions are not surveyed or a detailed historic-plan trace.
Neptune portrait/carved figural detail simplified to a relief panel with scrolls;
Corinthian capitals simplified, not claimed sculptural replicas. Hidden interior,
precise five-story partial parcel/roof reconciliation, complete rooftop furniture,
renovation infill facade and exact room/floor organization remain unverified.
USGS 2017 roof grid reaches 109 m and common roof cells 85.5–89.5 m, conflicting
with published 102.9 m tip. Published height is used; roof-grid discrepancy remains
unresolved rather than adding the old generic 14 m cupola above its maximum.
