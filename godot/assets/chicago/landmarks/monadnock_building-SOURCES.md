# Monadnock Building: original authored exterior

Original Blender geometry by this project, CC0-1.0. No third-party model or
photographic facade distributed. Canonical authoring script
`tools/blender/chicago_monadnock_building.py`; editable `.blend` in its authored folder.

References consulted 2026-10-03:
- [Building owner](https://monadnockbuilding.com/): current north corner / long
  west facade photograph inspected in browser.
- [Chicago Architecture Center](https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/monadnock-building):
  current entrance and south oriel/cornice photos inspected. North plain brick,
  flared base, cantilevered curved bays; south steel frame, ornamental terra cotta.
- [HABS ILL-1027](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/il/il0000/il0049/data/il0049data.pdf),
  pp. 2–3, 6–7: 215-foot height, 16 floors plus attic, dark brown narrow-joint
  brick, progressively chamfered corners, two north bay tiers and five east/west
  tiers, double-hung single-light wooden sash, thin iron sills, flared cornice,
  70-by-420-foot overall envelope including additions.

Model follows mapped w73671128 plan (about 20 by 122 m) rather than widening
into roads to match the nominal HABS overall envelope. Masonry top
65.532 m follows the HABS 215-foot description, not the map's 72-m height;
stylized roof skylights extend another .62 m, to 66.152 m.
North and south halves have separate bay profiles and cornices. Four entrance
names follow CAC's historic building divisions. Small details, bay spacing,
floor heights, rear elevations and rooftop skylights are stylized approximations;
this is not a surveyed current exterior. Source photographs are references only.
