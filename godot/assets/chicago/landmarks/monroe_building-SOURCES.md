# Monroe Building — authored exterior

Canonical Blender source: tools/blender/chicago_monroe_building.py. Original
geometry released as CC0; no embedded facade photos or downloaded third-party
model. The runtime replaces mapped footprint w145498713 once.

Primary references:
- Owner history: https://www.themonroebuilding.com/about ; Holabird & Roche,
  restored gables, Rookwood lobby tiles and Ludowici Spanish tile roof.
- Owner gallery: https://www.themonroebuilding.com/photogallery ; historic
  construction photo and restored east facade photograph inspected in browser,
  not redistributed. East frontage has five paired-window bays and a triangular
  gable with round-head windows; long north side carries separate paired bays.
- Restoration architect: https://www.holabird.com/monroe-building-historic-renovation-and-restoration ; restored roof profiles and custom tiles.
- Former museum occupant: https://www.pritzkermilitary.org/visit/historic-monroe-building-104-s-michigan-avenue ; 1912, 16 stories, terracotta/granite and replicated cast-iron entrances.

Mapped footprint w145498713: x[-73.8,-19.1], z[451.9,480.4], centre
(-46.45,street 8,466.15). Current mapped roof h74 m; not independently verified
published height. W54.4 x D27.4, eave64/ridge74, 14 office rows plus
street level and attic; placement yaw .02767 rad. Imported bounds start
(-27.71,-8,-14.21), size (55.42,82.11891,28.42): foundation extends below
street and the cornice projects .51 m. Roof trim reaches 74.11891 m.
133,002 triangles, 14 imported surfaces and 12 distinct materials.

Separate paired glazing, stone piers, spandrels, carved arch surrounds,
gable apertures, dormers, green roof courses and entrance vestibules are
physical geometry. Owner gallery restored street view and 77 East Monroe
entrance detail informed roof colour and turquoise entry tiles. The east
104 South Michigan entrance uses that same treatment as an approximation;
its exact position/design was not independently verified. Ironwork, reliefs,
pane spacing and proportions are simplified from photos, not historic plans.
Detailed figural sculpture and the Rookwood lobby vault are not reproduced.
Native day/night views and depth probes review placement and actual recesses;
they do not establish an exact architectural replica or whole-city acceptance.
