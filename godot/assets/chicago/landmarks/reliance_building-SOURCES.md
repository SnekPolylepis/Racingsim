# Reliance Building: original authored exterior

Original Blender geometry by this project, CC0-1.0. No source photo pixels or
third-party model distributed. Editable .blend and canonical author script:
`tools/blender/authored/reliance_building.blend` and
`tools/blender/chicago_reliance_building.py`.

References consulted 2026-10-03:
- [Chicago Architecture Center](https://www.architecture.org/online-resources/buildings-of-chicago/reliance-building):
  current exterior photograph inspected in browser; white terra cotta/glass,
  reconstructed cornice, projecting bays and retail podium. Image not distributed.
- [HABS ILL-1029, National Park Service / Library of Congress](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/il/il0000/il0041/data/il0041data.pdf),
  pp. 7–9: 55 ft 10 in by 84 ft 10 in, 200 ft height; narrow colonettes,
  Gothic spandrels, granite/bronze base, broad fixed pane with angled sash,
  north/east projecting bays, rear masonry and southwest light well.
- [OSM actual historic footprint w124865461](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/124865461),
  full-node API read, tags identify Reliance at 1 West Washington. Mapped ring
  ~26.9 x 17.7 m, center ~(-317.6,195.65). Neighbor w145625877 shares a Reliance
  alias but outlines a much larger adjacent block; its incorrect white terra-cotta
  override was transferred to the historic footprint. Neighbor massing retained.

Model envelope follows HABS dimensions (25.8572 x 17.018 m), architectural top
60.96 m; real projecting glass/sash, terra-cotta colonettes, physical quatrefoil
spandrels, restrained projecting cornice and retail glazing/name lettering.
HABS counts 15 stories, CAC 14; model includes street level and 14 window bands
(mezzanine included). Floor divisions, small tracery and retail content are
stylized approximations, not a claim of surveyed present-day dimensions.
Selective occupied glazing responds to the game's night setting.
