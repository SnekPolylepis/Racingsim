# 111 South Wacker: original authored exterior

Original project geometry, CC0-1.0. Canonical Blender script
`tools/blender/chicago_wacker_111.py`, editable `.blend` in its authored folder.
No third-party models or photographic facade pixels distributed.

References inspected 2026-10-03:
- [Goettsch Partners, architect](https://www.gpchicago.com/architecture/111-south-wacker/):
  built west/east, crown, cable-glass lobby and interior photographs inspected.
  Stainless V-shaped curtain mullions, broad column cladding, stepped roof,
  open base with round piers, clear cable glass, radial paving and ceiling patterns.
- [James Goettsch, 2012 CTBUH paper](https://global.ctbuh.org/resources/papers/download/265-how-tall-buildings-meet-the-ground-is-as-important-as-how-they-meet-the-sky.pdf),
  pp. 363–364: built circular parking ramp informs lobby/lighting/paving; white
  marble core, perimeter columns transferred to two widely spaced supports per
  side, and non-reflective cable glass. Early pedestal scheme is historical
  design context, not the built model. Architect website counts seven parking
  floors; paper describes eight. This asset approximates the visible envelope.
- [CVU / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/111-south-wacker/1528):
  207.6 m architectural height, 203.8 m highest occupied floor, 51 floors.
  [Building owner](https://www.111southwacker.com/the-building) also states 51;
  architect project page states 53. No surveyed floor schedule inferred.

Mapped w64887962 envelope: approximately 49.4 by 56.8 m, with 9.2 m western
projection on the central 47.4 m of the frontage. Native street origin/yaw follows
mapped north edge. Center top follows CVU; 201.4 m shoulder roofs, 13.8 m lobby,
23.7 m glass radius, 9.1–13.1 m helical underside, floor spacing, piers and
mechanical equipment are reference-informed proportions, not measured sections.
An 8 m foundation meets ground below raised Wacker street; garage interior is
not authored. V mullions, spiral soffit, net fittings, clear panes and entry parts
are real geometry. Selective offices, warm inner walls and the spiral light rim
respond to night mode. Unseen elevations and material colors remain stylized.
