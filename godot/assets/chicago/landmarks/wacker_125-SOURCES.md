# 125 South Wacker / Northern Trust Building: original authored exterior

Original project geometry, CC0-1.0. Canonical Blender script
`tools/blender/chicago_wacker_125.py`, editable `.blend` in its authored folder.
No third-party models or facade photographs redistributed.

References inspected 2026-10-03:
- [Menashe Properties, owner](https://menasheproperties.com/properties/office/125-south-wacker/):
  two west/south exterior photographs show grey granite vertical ribs, recessed
  bronze-framed vision panes, dark horizontal grille bands, a flat parapet and
  recessed lower arcade. Owner records 31 stories, 1974 completion and a 2019
  facade/lobby renovation. Its brochure link actually leads to 230 West Monroe;
  that unrelated brochure was not used as a 125-Wacker plan.
- [Official building site, Stream Realty / Menashe](https://125swacker.com/):
  current gallery exterior/entrance/lobby photographs show a glass canopy with
  raised address letters, clear entry, stone piers, white interior column and
  elevator walls, warm wood ceiling, linear lights and blue lounge seats.
- [CVU / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/northern-trust-building/15664):
  126.5 m architectural height, 31 floors, 1974, designed by Perkins+Will.
- [Monthly Bank News, October 1971, p. 55, Federal Reserve FRASER archive](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/monthlybanknews/monthlybanknews_197110.pdf):
  contemporary announcement describes granite/glass exterior and a main floor
  recessed along Wacker and Adams. It distinguishes this building from the
  bank's retained main offices at 50 South LaSalle. Search-index article text
  inspected; full PDF fetch unavailable. Owner's modern terminology differs.

Mapped w147350191: approximately 26.3 by 59.2 m main rectangle plus eastern
service projection; origin (-998.175, 564.75), yaw .019009. Existing measured roof
grid has a broad 123.5–124 m deck, central/rear rises and a maximum of 141.5 m.
The office facade/parapet uses the published 126.5 m; separate stepped rear
service/plant geometry preserves the measured height evidence rather than
stretching every vision-glass floor to the old generic 141.5 m height. The
registry/roof-grid height difference is unresolved; interpretation of the higher
rear cells as plant, its louvers and exact enclosure dimensions are approximate.
No claim that the full mechanical roof was surveyed or photographically verified.
Mapped outline is retained for the 8 m foundation and tower backing. Main lobby
6.4 m, arcade/glass setback 4 m, canopy, rib widths, floor spacing, grille rows,
interior layout and furnishings are reference-informed proportions. Existing
city landscaping remains. Tenant logos, unseen service rooms and garage interior
are not reproduced. Granite/bronze colors remain stylized; deep ribs and separate
panes are actual geometry. Restrained glass/stone highlights replace generic glare.
