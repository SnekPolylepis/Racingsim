# 155 North Wacker: original authored exterior

Original Blender geometry by this project, CC0-1.0. No downloaded models or
photographic facade pixels distributed. Canonical `tools/blender/chicago_wacker_155.py`
and editable `tools/blender/authored/wacker_155.blend`.

References inspected 2026-10-03:
- [Goettsch Partners, architect](https://www.gpchicago.com/architecture/155-north-wacker/):
  H-shaped tower, 45-foot south arcade, two-story lobby and cable-supported glazing.
  Published built exterior, river and lobby photographs inspected in browser.
  Modeled projecting vertical blades, fine curtain mullions, open colonnade,
  oversized lobby panes, metal cable fittings, warm interior walls and ceiling strips.
- [CVU / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/155-north-wacker/1862):
  architectural top 194.6 m, highest occupied floor 178.8 m; 45 above-ground floors.
- [Building owner](https://www.155northwacker.com/): full-height glazing, column-free
  floor plates and 13-foot slab spacing. Published floor counts differ: architect
  48, owner 46, CVU 45; model does not infer a surveyed floor schedule from these.

Mapped w136662656 envelope approximated by a 66.5 by 54.2 m plan. H recesses,
wing roofs at 191.6 m, lower bridge at 188.6 m, 8 m arcade depth, piers and
pane spacing are photograph-derived proportions, not measured construction drawings.
Arcade clear height follows architect's 45 feet (13.716 m); upper blade tips
follow the published architectural height. Two-sided clear lobby glazing encloses
modeled walls and entry parts; dielectric tower glass avoids generic facade glare.
An 8 m foundation meets city ground below the raised Wacker street; it does not
model an accessible garage. Mechanical equipment, colors and unseen elevations are stylized. Existing adjacent
park scenery is retained; this asset does not claim a surveyed pocket-park rebuild.
