# 191 North Wacker: original authored exterior

Original Blender geometry by this project, CC0-1.0. No third-party models or
photographic facade pixels distributed. Canonical authoring script
`tools/blender/chicago_wacker_191.py`; editable `.blend` in its authored folder.

References consulted 2026-10-03:
- [KPF, architect](https://www.kpf.com/project/191-north-wacker-drive): built
  river-side exterior and original lobby photographs inspected in browser.
  Rectangular dark reflective tower, fine vertical west mullions, stronger
  horizontal north bands, recessed lobby with piers and revolving doors.
  Lantern explicitly comprises a solid inner volume and clear outer sleeve.
  Illustration treated as design context, not a surveyed built elevation.
- [CVU / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/191-north-wacker-drive/3530):
  157.4 m architectural height, 135 m highest occupied floor, 37 above-ground floors.
- [Building owner](https://www.191northwacker.com/about): 37 floors, typical
  25,000-square-foot floorplate. [Current home page](https://www.191northwacker.com/)
  notes ongoing lobby/signage improvements; the model approximates the architect's
  published exterior, not unverified completion of those current alterations.

Plan follows mapped w147013355 (~42.3 by 54.2 m). Main roof 143 m and lantern
division/width/depth are photograph-derived approximations within the published
157.4 m height, not measured sections. Mullions, glazing bands, canopy, crown
frames and entry parts are real geometry; selective office and lantern emission
respond to the game's night setting. Clear sleeve has a separate transparent
material around the inner volume. Rear elevations, pane spacing and mechanical
equipment are stylized; colors encode visible material categories, not calibrated
photographic samples. Source photos remain references only.
