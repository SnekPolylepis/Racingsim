# 71 South Wacker: original authored exterior

Original project geometry, CC0-1.0. Canonical Blender script
`tools/blender/chicago_wacker_71.py`, editable `.blend` in its authored folder.
No third-party models or photographic facade pixels distributed.

References inspected 2026-10-03:
- [Pei Cobb Freed & Partners, design architect](https://www.pcf-p.com/projects/hyatt-center/):
  exterior/end-view/aerial photographs, site plan and lobby photographs inspected.
  Bowed lozenge plan, silver horizontal bands, broad end blades and recessed glass
  spines, twin entrance canopies, granite core, curved lobby and bamboo planters.
  Architect gives 50 ft (15.24 m) end reception halls and 36 ft (10.9728 m) main
  lobby, with a garden along the south side. Site plan informs the narrower
  upper tower, rather than extending the compound footprint to the roof.
- [CVU / Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/71-south-wacker/1546):
  207.1 m architectural height, 201 m highest occupied floor, 48 floors, 2005.
- [Epstein, architect of record](https://www.epsteinglobal.com/projects/hyatt-center-high-rise-office-building):
  48-story building on the Wacker/Monroe/Franklin/Arcade Place half block.

Mapped compound w148685510 sets ground foundation and origin (-964.95, 423.4),
with .019526 rad alignment. Upper lens: approximately 94 by 45.2 m, parabolic
curves approximating the photographed bowed elevations; 2 m inset end slots.
Upper floors, blade widths, glass panes, ceilings, canopies, core and garden beds
are reference-informed proportions, not surveyed construction dimensions.
An 8 m foundation meets ground under raised street. Real panes, bands, piers,
reception vestibules, granite core, ceiling strips and bamboo replace the generic
tall block. Existing city trees remain. Roof/garage interiors and detailed skylight
structure are not reconstructed; decorative mural artwork is not reproduced.
Dielectric glass and restrained silver band highlights avoid the previous generic
facade glare. Office panes, reception walls and ceiling strips toggle at night.
Colors and unseen elevations remain stylized.
