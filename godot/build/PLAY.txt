RACING SIM - WINDOWS

Double-click RacingSim.exe. No installation, browser or Godot editor needed.

WASD/arrows: drive. Space: handbrake. R: reset. Q/E: shift. C: clutch.
M: automatic/manual. V: camera. B/Y: debug/telemetry. F11: fullscreen.
G: garage. Esc: close menu or pause.
Hold brake at rest in automatic mode to switch forward/reverse.

Controller: left stick steering, RT/LT pedals, LB clutch, X handbrake,
A/B shift, Back reset, Start pause. Remap controls in Settings.

All three bundled circuits, three cars, 42 tuning fields, lap ghosts, minimap,
telemetry, recorded engine audio and procedural driving effects.
Settings includes audio volume and mute controls.

Garage saves named setups. Best laps save ghost telemetry.
Default saves: %APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Racing Sim/

Godot engine license and dependency notices accompany this executable.

HELP: choose a chapter in the in-game Player handbook for detailed driving,
garage, files and troubleshooting instructions. Source maintainers
and LLMs should start with AGENTS.md, then godot/docs/LLM-GUIDE.md.

CONSOLE PRESENTATION: Default pairing is Spa-Francorchamps and Ferrari 296 GT3,
with Simcade, Afternoon and 640x448 Authentic UI. Press Start/Enter on the title,
then choose Race, car and circuit. D-pad/arrows move; A/Enter selects; B/Esc backs
out. Shoulder buttons or Page Up/Down switch settings/garage tabs. Mouse works
too, including the HUD Pause button. The idle title runs a driving demonstration.

Settings offers 480p component, optional 480i fields, CRT/composite, 4:3/16:9,
Sharp UI, 720p/Native, speed blur and Simulation handling.
TCS 0-10, ASM 0-10 and ABS On/Off; Simcade defaults to 3/3/On. Handling models
keep separate best laps; lighting is cosmetic. Pause > Time sheet shows lap
results and last-lap replay. All assets are local; no runtime internet access.

Rajdhani OFL and Poly Haven CC0 license texts accompany the engine notices.
CC0 Ferrari engine recordings by jtvdb and biholao are edited into RPM/load loops.
Recorded models are unspecified; these are not exact 296 GT3 engine recordings.
The source docs/PS2-FOLLOWUP-REPORT.md records tests and comparison evidence.
