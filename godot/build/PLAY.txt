RACING SIM - REBUILD PREVIEW 8 (2026-09-28)

A preliminary build of the rebuilt game: a full 3-D car on hand-built 3-D circuits. It is a
preview for testing, not a finished release. No installation, browser or Godot editor needed.

START
  Windows: double-click RacingSim.exe.   macOS: open Racing Sim.app (see MACOS below).
  In the menu choose Race, then a car and a circuit, then Load circuit. The first time a circuit is opened it is built
  (Spa takes about half a minute, the full Nordschleife and Chicago longer) and cached, so later loads are quick. Esc pauses: resume,
  restart the lap, settings, garage, or back to the menu.

CIRCUITS
  Proving Ground  - an invented test circuit: a banked bowl, a crest you can take off from
                    at ~150 km/h, a compression, off-camber turns, a concrete ditch and every
                    kind of kerb.
  Spa-Francorchamps - built from real survey data: the OpenStreetMap centreline, SPW LiDAR
                    elevation and road banking, and road widths and kerbs measured from SPW's
                    2023 aerial photos.
  Nurburgring Nordschleife - the whole 20.8 km lap from the OpenStreetMap centreline and
                    Rhineland-Palatinate 1 m terrain, with the Karussell banking, Flugplatz,
                    Pflanzgarten and the Doettinger Hoehe straight.
  Chicago - River & Lake - an 8.1 km lap of downtown Chicago built from OpenStreetMap: Michigan
                    Avenue, Jackson Drive, Lake Shore Drive, and Lower and Upper Wacker Drive
                    at two levels, past the Bean, Willis Tower, Navy Pier and the river bridges.
                    Try Afterhours for the lit skyline and the lamps under Lower Wacker.
  Nordschleife (T13 - Aremberg) - the first section of the Nurburgring Nordschleife, from the
                    start to Aremberg, built from Rhineland-Palatinate 1 m terrain (DGM1) and
                    the OpenStreetMap centreline. A point-to-point section; the rest follows.

CARS
  Mazda MX-5 (NA 1.6), GT (high downforce), Ferrari 296 GT3, Ferrari F2004 and Red Bull RB19, chosen in the menu
  (Race > Car), each with its own PS2-era model and working head and tail lamps at night.
  Settings > Driving picks Simcade (assisted) or Simulation handling and the aids (TCS, ASM,
  ABS); the Garage edits the setup. Records are kept per car, setup and handling model.

DRIVING
  W/Up throttle, S/Down brake, A/D or Left/Right steer, Space handbrake, C clutch,
  E/Shift and Q/Ctrl shift up/down, R reset to the grid.
  V change camera (chase, high chase, bonnet, two overhead), Y telemetry, B debug.
  Esc pauses. Controllers work too (left stick, triggers, bumpers); rebind in Settings > Controls.

NEW: MONACO
  Circuit de Monaco - the 3.3 km Grand Prix lap on the real streets: Sainte-Devote, the climb to
  Casino Square, Mirabeau, the Fairmont hairpin, the tunnel, the chicane, the Swimming Pool and
  Rascasse, among Monaco's real buildings.

NEW IN THE PREVIEW 8 REFRESH
  - Two new cars: the Ferrari F2004 and the Red Bull RB19.
  - Chicago: L trains running on the elevated tracks, boats and piers on the lake, photo
    facades on Michigan Avenue, parked cars, and a wet NFS-style night by default.
  - Sharper picture (native resolution with anti-aliasing) and a new chase camera.
  - Monza and the old Nordschleife T13-Aremberg section are gone; the full Nordschleife stays.
  - New car and circuit menus: a list with details for each; Enter picks.
  - F2004 and RB19 handle like formula cars (7 and 8 gears, strong brakes); the RB19 is navy.
  - Lighter rain.

NEW IN PREVIEW 8
  - Ferrari 296 GT3: a detailed model in the Verstappen Racing livery, with its real wheels.
  - Chicago: the real Willis Tower and Bean, Chicago-style signal poles, hydrants, parking meters,
    bus stops, dumpsters, road-works clutter on Lower Wacker, manholes and a night skyline backdrop.
  - Nordschleife: grass, bushes, stones and mossy boulders along the verges.
  - Nights: street lamps are real lights now; they reflect softly off the damp road instead of
    painted streaks, and the moon no longer glares on the tarmac.

NEW IN PREVIEW 7
  - Chicago: a full city circuit with a real street grid, buildings with day and night windows,
    shopfronts, traffic signals, crosswalks, the L, bridges, lake, parks and street props.
  - The complete Nurburgring Nordschleife, 20.8 km, with the Karussell as a banked concrete bowl.
    Kerbs on the full lap are only where the corner data marks them; traced kerbs are next.
  - Calmer nights: the wet-road glare, headlights and lamp streaks are toned down so the road
    reads as damp asphalt instead of a cream sheet. Lower Wacker has rows of amber lamps.
  - Spa and Nordschleife haze and grass colour tuned against the references.
  - New Kenney particle sprites for smoke, dust, grass, gravel, sparks and backfires.

NEW IN PREVIEW 6
  - NFS Underground-style nights: wet-road light streaks, glowing trackside buildings, and a
    night sky that is never black.
  - Mixed forests with undergrowth: beech, oak, birch and spruce, ferns, brambles, long verge
    grass and hedges (Flugplatz).
  - Kerbs traced from aerial photos at Spa and the Nordschleife, placed where the paint really is.
  - Braking boards (300/200/100 m) before Spa's braking corners.
  - Tyre smoke, grass and gravel spray, sparks from walls and scrapes, and exhaust backfires.
  - Nordschleife road undulation from the 1 m laser survey (5 m sampling).

NEW IN PREVIEW 5
  - Real Mazda MX-5 NA body with pop-up lights.
  - Photographic spruce, fir, deciduous trees and bushes; wooded hill skylines and fog to ~1 km.
  - Sharp 2K photo asphalt, repair patches, tar seams and white road-edge lines.
  - Double Armco rails on dark posts, like GT4; an enclosed Nordschleife S1 with forest banks.

NEW IN PREVIEW 4
  - New daylight shading and trackside colour at Spa and the Nordschleife.
  - Track scenery now appears after headless builds; circuits rebuild once with scenery.
  - Nordschleife is selectable; the settings panel fits. Wall corners use each wall's contact.
  - About 75% fewer car and scenery draw calls (Spa: 575 to 136 per view).

NEW IN PREVIEW 3
  - Amber nights: sodium lamps, road glow and headlights that light the road.
  - 640x448 PS2 renderer with glow and motion persistence, CRT and 480i options, and Authentic UI.
  - Crowds, grandstands, catch fences, tree cards, billboards, gantries and marshal posts.

NEW IN PREVIEW 2
  - Nordschleife section 1 (T13 to Aremberg).
  - Dedicated car models for all three cars, with lamps that light after dark.
  - Settings (display, driving, controls, audio), Garage and a Pause menu on the new front end.
  - PS2-style road and ground textures on every circuit; Afternoon / Afterhours time of day.
  - The old pre-rebuild game has been removed from the source; this is the only game now.

WHAT'S NEW IN THE REBUILD
  - The car is a true 3-D body on four suspension corners, with tyre flex and wheel mass:
    it can crest, fly, land, roll over and ride kerbs realistically.
  - Walls, barriers and knock-over cones; lap timing through 3-D gates with sectors and a
    live delta; best-lap ghost; minimap; tyre temperatures; telemetry; engine, tyre and impact audio.
  - Best laps, ghosts and sector times save per car, setup and handling model.

KNOWN LIMITS OF THIS PREVIEW
  - The Miata's night tail-glow sits slightly low on the bumper.
  - The full Nordschleife's kerbs are a stopgap (only flagged corners have them), and its
    runoff and scenery are less detailed than Spa's.
  - Chicago is an authored racing route on real street geometry, not a surveyed city model; two
    ramp connectors are invented. Its first load and its draw load are heavier than the other circuits.
  - Runoff areas at Spa are approximations.
  - Records from the old game do not carry over (they live in a separate folder).
