RACING SIM - REBUILD PREVIEW 2 (2026-09-24)

A preliminary build of the rebuilt game: a full 3-D car on hand-built 3-D circuits. It is a
preview for testing, not a finished release. No installation, browser or Godot editor needed.

START
  Windows: double-click RacingSim.exe.   macOS: open Racing Sim.app (see MACOS below).
  In the menu choose Race, then a car and a circuit, then Load circuit. The first time a circuit is opened it is built
  (Spa takes about half a minute) and cached, so later loads are quick. Esc pauses: resume,
  restart the lap, settings, garage, or back to the menu.

CIRCUITS
  Proving Ground  - an invented test circuit: a banked bowl, a crest you can take off from
                    at ~150 km/h, a compression, off-camber turns, a concrete ditch and every
                    kind of kerb.
  Spa-Francorchamps - built from real survey data: the OpenStreetMap centreline, SPW LiDAR
                    elevation and road banking, and road widths and kerbs measured from SPW's
                    2023 aerial photos.
  Nordschleife (T13 - Aremberg) - the first section of the Nurburgring Nordschleife, from the
                    start to Aremberg, built from Rhineland-Palatinate 1 m terrain (DGM1) and
                    the OpenStreetMap centreline. A point-to-point section; the rest follows.

CARS
  Mazda MX-5 (NA 1.6), GT (high downforce) and the Ferrari 296 GT3, chosen in the menu
  (Race > Car), each with its own PS2-era model and working head and tail lamps at night.
  Settings > Driving picks Simcade (assisted) or Simulation handling and the aids (TCS, ASM,
  ABS); the Garage edits the setup. Records are kept per car, setup and handling model.

DRIVING
  W/Up throttle, S/Down brake, A/D or Left/Right steer, Space handbrake, C clutch,
  E/Shift and Q/Ctrl shift up/down, R reset to the grid.
  V change camera (chase, high chase, bonnet, two overhead), Y telemetry, B debug.
  Esc pauses. Controllers work too (left stick, triggers, bumpers); rebind in Settings > Controls.

NEW IN PREVIEW 2
  - Nordschleife section 1 (T13 to Aremberg).
  - Dedicated car models for all three cars, with lamps that light after dark.
  - Settings (display, driving, controls, audio), Garage and a Pause menu on the new front end.
  - PS2-style road and ground textures on every circuit; Afternoon / Afterhours time of day.
  - The old pre-rebuild game has been removed from the source; this is the only game now.

WHAT'S NEW IN THE REBUILD
  - The car is a true 3-D body on four suspension corners, with tyre flex and wheel mass:
    it can crest, fly, land, roll over and ride kerbs realistically.
  - Walls, barriers and knock-over cones; lap timing through 3-D gates with sectors and a
    live delta; best-lap ghost; minimap; tyre temperatures; telemetry; engine, tyre and impact audio.
  - Best laps, ghosts and sector times save per car, setup and handling model.

KNOWN LIMITS OF THIS PREVIEW
  - Only the first Nordschleife section; the rest of the lap is in progress.
  - Sparse trackside scenery (crowds, fences and gantries are being added), and Afterhours
    has no trackside floodlights or amber road glow yet.
  - Runoff areas at Spa are approximations.
  - Records from the old game do not carry over (they live in a separate folder).

SAVES
  %APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/Racing Sim/v2/   (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/
  Godot/app_userdata/Racing Sim/v2/). The old game's saves are left untouched.

MACOS
  The app is not notarized: the first time, right-click Racing Sim.app, choose Open, then
  Open again in the dialog.
