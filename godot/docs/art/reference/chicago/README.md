# Chicago reference board

Private visual research board for CHI-03. The photos are comparison targets, not game assets; no
geometry, textures, logos, or other game data is derived from them. Commons photographs were saved
as JPEG thumbnails no wider than 1,280 px. Attribute each file to its photographer and licence as
listed below. The YouTube frames are thumbnail images from cited PS2 gameplay videos and remain
copyrighted by their respective owners; they are retained only for private visual comparison.

exact photo reproductions.

## Camera mapping and observed gaps

Run `tests/v2/chicago_screenshots.gd` windowed with `-- --v2-flow-test --v2-track=chicago --compare`.
It saves 26 day/night views to `user://chicago-shots` by default (or `--out=<dir>`). Every spot
writes a real-photo strip and a separate closest-game-frame strip. `bonnet-lower-wacker` is the
bonnet camera; the two Loop aerial positions are elevated look-down comparison views from the
nearest road stations.

| Camera spot | Real reference | Game frame | Gap: buildings / street furniture / water / light / colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Michigan Avenue at the Bean | `chicago-real-michigan-bean-day.jpg`; night: Michigan bridge view | NFSU neon street | Bean is a simplified silhouette; few sidewalk details; no people; real stainless reflection absent; our night windows are sparser and amber. |
| Lake Shore Drive / lake | `chicago-real-lake-shore-day.jpg`; night: lakefront | NFSU wide road | Skyline is blockout-scale; real lake horizon is open; rail, signs and traffic are sparse; water sheen and sky tint differ. |
| Navy Pier | `chicago-real-navy-pier-day.jpg`; night: riverfront lights (nearest night water reference) | NFSU wet-road reflections | Pier and wheel are low-poly; no pedestrians/boats; lake edge lacks wakes; game lights are more saturated and streaked. |
| Upper Wacker | three-level Wacker day; Wacker night | NFSU wide road | Building faces repeat; curbside signs/parked cars are sparse; river reflection is broad and procedural; our lamps are evenly spaced. |
| Lower Wacker tunnel | Randolph exit day; tunnel night | NFSU neon street | Our tunnel supports and ceiling differ in rhythm; traffic/signs are absent; concrete and water are simplified; game glow is stronger. |
| Michigan Avenue bridge | Michigan Avenue bridge day/night | NFSU start grid | Bridgehouse details are simplified; no moving traffic or pedestrians; reflections have no photographed skyline structure; palette is more amber. |
| State Street bridge | State Street day; river night as closest night view | NFSU wet reflections | Bridge trusses and lift machinery are low-detail; sparse signs and traffic; river surface lacks reflected windows; night colour is more uniform. |
| LaSalle bridge | LaSalle Street day; riverfront night | NFSU wide road | Bascule geometry and sidewalk furniture are reduced; no traffic; reflection is procedural; city lights lack the photo's varied white/green balance. |
| Willis Tower from river | Willis Skydeck river view day/night surrogate | NFSU native city start | Tower shape and antenna are coarse; riverwalk furniture is sparse; no true local building reflection; our windows glow uniformly. |
| Wacker by Merchandise Mart | Mart and Wells bridge day; Wacker night | NFSU neon street | Mart's limestone articulation is simplified; bollards/railings are sparse; water is darker and smoother; night signs lack game-like neon variety. |
| Loop aerial north | Hancock Observatory Loop view | NFSU motion blur | Circuit camera cannot match the real height; procedural skyline silhouettes are fewer; street grids and traffic are absent; there is no aerial water texture detail. |
| Loop aerial river | Willis Skydeck river view | NFSU wet start grid | Camera is elevated from the road, not the source's rooftop height; skyline density differs; river glint is procedural; warm/cool balance differs. |
| Bonnet, Lower Wacker | Randolph exit day; Lower Wacker pillar at night | NFSU pre-release PS2 frame | Tunnel ceiling/support rhythm and signage differ; no traffic ahead; light pools are simpler; game image has more motion blur and wet highlights. |
