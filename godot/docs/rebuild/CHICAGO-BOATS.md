# Chicago moored-vessel source review

2026-10-01. No replacement vessel accepted yet.

## Map evidence

`assets/cc0-source/chicago/roadmap/harbor.json` has 649 mooring nodes:
383 buoys, 180 bollards, 83 piles and three posts. Generated data now retains
node ID, category, operator and exact previous local point. Buoys specify
attachment locations, not hull centres, occupancy, vessel type or dimensions.
The 383 remaining rendered vessels are provisional, including random heading,
scale, box geometry, guessed material and waterline. Do not mark boats complete
from the fixture-removal screenshots.

## Asset review

Local asset roots were searched by boat/sail/yacht/hull filenames; no boat model
identified. This is not proof that all internal meshes in every pack were reviewed.

[Low-Poly Sailboat by Kenepin](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-sailboat-fdf38588937242f5b85efc87e028a876)
was downloaded through the signed-in Sketchfab browser. Page and download modal
explicitly show CC BY 4.0, author credit required, commercial use allowed.
376,324-byte GLB remains in Downloads, not imported into the game or redistributed.
SHA-256: 7d68f0d3ddeb002f6d25b8bae99a6317042f2438b57ffb694ae4f54f3a2195e4.

Orbiting the viewer exposed raised triangular sails, stylized hull/portholes,
cyan/red materials and no specified physical dimensions. Reject for this harbor
replacement rather than reinterpret the author's model as a real Chicago vessel.

[Sailboat Ecume de Mer by rubidu](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sailboat-ecume-de-mer-4f4c81aadfb24594a0331ed9636e29e0)
was checked in the signed-in browser. Page has no download control or displayed
reuse licence. It is unavailable for this acquisition workflow; no viewer asset
extraction, download endpoint guessing or author message attempted.
