Racing Sim for macOS

Open Racing Sim.app. From the source workspace, Play Racing Sim.command opens
the same app. No Godot editor or network connection is needed to play.

WASD or arrows drive; Space handbrakes; R resets; Esc pauses; V changes camera.
E/Q shift, C clutches, M changes transmission, G opens the garage.
F2 opens the circuit editor; F11 toggles fullscreen. On Apple keyboards you
may need Fn/Globe with the function keys. Menus also expose these actions.
In the editor use Command+Z / Command+Shift+Z to undo/redo, Command+S to save.
Control shortcuts also work. Pinch zooms; Settings offers trackpad scroll pan.

Default saves: ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Racing Sim/
Circuits > Choose folder connects an existing tracks/setups/ghosts library.
See in-game Help for complete controls and file workflows.

This universal app contains Apple Silicon and Intel code. Local validation
and known limits are recorded in godot/docs/MACOS.md in the source project.
The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized for public distribution. A downloaded
copy may require approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Keep the accompanying engine and asset license notices with the package.
