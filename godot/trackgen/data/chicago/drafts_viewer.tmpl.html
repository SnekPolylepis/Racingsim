Chicago detour drafts

Same main roads as the current circuit, with real side streets swapped in for more corners. Every detour follows an OpenStreetMap way in its legal one-way direction.

    Side by side

    Corners count turns of 35° or more on the control polyline; the game rounds each one with a fillet of up to 55 m. ≈ 90° counts turns between 75° and 105°. Spa is about 7.0 km; the current Chicago route is 8.30 km.

    What to know before picking one
    • Real roads only. Street geometry is the OpenStreetMap extract in osm-roads.json plus osm-grid.json (cross streets, fetched 2026-09-28). Nothing was invented, but the two game-only connectors (harbor, south) are unchanged.
    • Lake Shore Drive has no real side streets in the loop, so only Drafts C and E touch the lakefront side, via Columbus Dr and Monroe Dr. E keeps only the 300 m of Lake Shore Dr between Jackson Dr and Monroe Dr; F and G drop it.
    • Shorter drafts (E, F, G). They drop the harbor connector and most of the south Wacker run. The Wacker turnaround is still a game-only link, redrawn as a hairpin, and the Lake Shore Dr to Monroe Dr exit is taken from the OSM road graph without a ramp survey. F and G leave out main roads (F: Jackson Dr and Lake Shore Dr; G: Lake Shore Dr).
    • Corner size. The weave uses 140 m blocks, so its 90° corners get full 55 m fillets with about 60 m of straight between them. Expect low-speed street-circuit corners rather than sweepers.
    • Not wired into the game yet. The drafts are route-a.json to route-g.json in the same format as route.json. Landmark corners in chicago.gd are indexed by point number, so switching the game to a draft needs that list remapped, a new cache revision and re-recorded lap times.
